Your website, and the systems behind it.

The website

Two ways to pay.

Pay once, or pay monthly for a year. Hosting and care is a separate add-on, on either option. The domain stays in your name.

Pay upfront

£1,495 One-off

You own the website from day one.

Hosting and care is not included. It is £39 a month on top, the same as if you pay monthly.

Pay monthly

£129 A month, for 12 months

Then you own the website outright.

If you stop paying early, the site comes offline. You keep your domain. Hosting and care is not included in the £129. It is £39 a month on top.

Add-on, either way

£39 a month

Hosting and care

With either way of paying for the website. Not included in the £129. It covers hosting, security, updates, small text and photo changes, and visitor analytics.

The domain is always registered in your name. That is true whether you pay upfront or monthly, and it stays yours if the monthly site comes offline.

On top of the website

The automated systems, in plain English.

These sit alongside the website, on a monthly plan. Each one does a job that otherwise waits until you have a spare minute.

AI Voice Employee
Answers the phone and books the call, 24/7. If you are on a job, or it is after hours, the call is still answered and the details are taken.
24/7 AI Chatbot
Lives on the website, for people who would rather type than ring. It answers, and takes the enquiry, at any hour.
Database Reactivation
Goes back to old leads and past customers and wins that conversation back, instead of leaving it sitting there.
Review Accelerator
After the job, it asks the customer for a Google review. The point of it is more 5-star reviews.
Referral Flywheel
Asks a happy customer to pass your name on. A referral comes in as a new enquiry, and can run through the same path.
Lifetime Value System
Sends servicing reminders, for example when an annual boiler service is due, and brings repeat work back to you.
